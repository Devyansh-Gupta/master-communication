# Formal ASD-STE100 review

Load this reference only for an explicit formal compliance or rule-audit request. Ordinary technical communication uses the STE-inspired guidance in SKILL.md.

1. Establish the applicable edition, document scope, and domain terminology.
2. Obtain the official standard and dictionary through an authorized source: https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html
3. Check vocabulary, approved meanings, parts of speech, permitted forms, writing rules, and authorized technical terms. Use the standard's word-count method, not a generic tokenizer.
4. Compare the revision with its source. Preserve uncertainty, permissions, recommendations, requirements, logical conditions, and timing. If a compliant equivalent cannot be established, report the unresolved departure instead of changing meaning.
5. Report the original passage, supported issue, proposed revision, and reason. Cite rule numbers only after checking the applicable standard.

If the standard or dictionary is unavailable, offer a limited structural review and identify missing coverage. Short sentences, a domain glossary, and a clean linter result do not establish formal compliance. Do not invent dictionary rulings or call an incomplete review certified.

This is an unofficial aid, not ASD or STEMG endorsement. It does not reproduce the official dictionary. Preserve exact code and quotations while reviewing their surrounding prose.
